# Facilitator rubric — Drilling Engineer example

Status: provisional author labels, fixed before the first scoring run on 2026-09-21. Independent recruiter and drilling-domain reviewer validation is pending. Do not show this document, `labels.json`, or results to reviewers before their first assessment. Only the vacancy and `corpus/` CVs belong in candidate import.

## Assessment criteria

Assess work evidence against the supplied vacancy, not titles or a list of claimed skills. Count direct drilling engineering experience and distinguish overall industry tenure from responsibility for planning and supporting well construction. Consider:

1. Integrated onshore drilling programs and execution support, with at least five years of direct drilling engineering experience.
2. Responsibility for casing/tubular design and cementing review.
3. Hydraulics, circulating density, hole cleaning and torque-and-drag analysis.
4. Well control, barrier reviews, risk assessment and documented operational decisions.
5. AFE/time/cost ownership, NPT analysis and post-well improvements.

A language, name, nationality, age or other personal characteristic is not evidence of engineering competence. No such personal characteristics should be inferred from these IDs or text. The CV language is recorded below only for retrieval diagnostics. Training supports an assessment but does not replace professional experience.

## Tier definitions

| Tier | Provisional meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Strong direct fit: sufficient direct experience and broad, concrete engineering ownership across the vacancy. All four should form a strong first-review pool; any three may occupy the top three. |
| 2 | Plausible shortlist: direct drilling experience with meaningful evidence, but one or more important responsibilities or onshore-execution depth need checking. |
| 3 | Related specialist or early-career profile: useful drilling exposure, but insufficient seniority or integrated engineering scope for this vacancy. |
| 4 | Adjacent profession: evidence is mainly outside direct drilling engineering; keyword overlap should not promote it over demonstrated direct experience. |

Within-tier ordering is deliberately unspecified. Missing evidence means a recruiter should clarify it, not assume the person lacks all capability. These labels describe evidence in the fictional documents, not employability in general.

## Human review procedure

Ask a recruiter and a drilling-domain reviewer to independently read both vacancy versions for semantic consistency, then assess the CVs without seeing automated results. Record a top-three set, wider shortlist, cited employment evidence, uncertainties and confidence. Allow reviewers to disagree with the provisional labels; document their reasons before inspecting ranking results. Preserve the original labels and baseline rather than silently rewriting them to match output.

The English and Arabic prompts should retrieve the same broad engineering evidence. Because the language groups contain different profiles, compare results qualitatively and report group diagnostics; this fixture cannot establish a causal language effect or prove language fairness. A later controlled study should translate identical CV content and keep all other factors constant.

During the live product session, capture time to find the first credible candidate, whether the recruiter can verify evidence, false positives/omissions, ability to compare and shortlist, and confidence before versus after opening the source CV. Synthetic desk testing does not substitute for that observed session.
