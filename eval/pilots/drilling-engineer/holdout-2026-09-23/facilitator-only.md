## Label rubric

- Tier 1: substantial job-history evidence of integrated well planning and drilling operations support, with the requested experience length and several relevant engineering disciplines represented.
- Tier 2: substantial direct drilling-service evidence, but the stated scope concentrates on a specialist component of the integrated role.
- Tier 3: substantial adjacent oil-and-gas engineering experience with some relevant inputs to well planning; career responsibilities center on a different discipline.
- Tier 4: substantial engineering experience in a different industry application. Shared technical vocabulary does not establish oil-and-gas well-engineering responsibility.

The tiers express the intended relative relevance to this one posting. They are not a universal hierarchy of occupations or individuals. Missing responsibilities mean the text supplies no evidence for them; they do not establish that a fictional person lacks the ability. Tier 1 does not designate a single winner, and no within-tier ordering is labelled.
