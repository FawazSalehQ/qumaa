Well Planning and Drilling Engineer

We need an engineer to prepare integrated oil and gas well plans and support their execution. The role covers both planning and operations; it is not limited to one service discipline.

Responsibilities
- Review offset-well records, subsurface objectives, formation pressures and operational lessons to establish the well design basis.
- Develop directional trajectories and coordinate survey and anti-collision reviews with the relevant specialists.
- Select casing setting depths and perform casing load checks; integrate cementing objectives and placement plans with the well design.
- Develop the drilling fluid and hydraulics basis, including hole-cleaning and equivalent circulating density considerations, with fluids specialists.
- Assess drillstring and bottom-hole assembly options, torque and drag, and equipment constraints.
- Prepare drilling programs, time and cost estimates, risk registers, contingency plans and documented well-control considerations for technical review.
- Support the rig team during execution, investigate departures from the plan, and route proposed changes through the technical approval process.
- Use daily operational data to investigate nonproductive time and update future plans; write end-of-well reports and coordinate across subsurface, rig and service teams.

Experience and preparation
- At least five years of relevant engineering experience, with evidence of both well planning and drilling operations support.
- A degree in petroleum engineering, mechanical engineering or another closely relevant engineering discipline.
- Demonstrated contributions across several well-engineering disciplines, including casing, directional planning, fluids or hydraulics, and operational risk.
- Practical experience with engineering calculations or well-planning software and clear technical documentation.

Assessment will focus on responsibilities and work evidence. No particular software brand, professional certification, nationality or language background is required by this posting.
