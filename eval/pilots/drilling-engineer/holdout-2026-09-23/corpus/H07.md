## Experience

January 2022–August 2026

- Prepared foundation designs for 9 building projects using geotechnical investigation reports and structural load schedules supplied by the project teams.

- Prepared material quantities, construction sequence notes and design risk registers. Assessed proposed site changes and issued revised drawings after technical review.
- Investigated two concrete placement delays with the contractor and recorded changes to delivery scheduling for subsequent foundation pours.
