## Professional profile

Drilling and well delivery engineer with experience from January 2018 to August 2026 on mature oil-field redevelopment. Has planned new wells and sidetracks, supported operations and used post-well reviews to improve later engineering packages.

## Experience

### Well Delivery Engineer — Meridian Field Engineering
January 2022–August 2026

- Prepared engineering programs for 7 horizontal wells and 5 sidetracks. Compared offset records with the new target requirements and documented uncertainties in the initial design basis.
- Evaluated kickoff and landing alternatives with the directional team. Reviewed survey uncertainty and anti-collision findings before incorporating the selected path into the program.
- Designed casing and liner arrangements, selected setting depths, and checked burst, collapse and tension load cases. Coordinated cementing objectives and placement constraints for each arrangement.
- Calculated drilling hydraulics and circulating density for the planned fluid properties. Checked hole-cleaning margins and compared flow requirements with the available pumps.
- Assessed torque and drag and bottom-hole assembly options for the planned trajectories. Worked with the rig team to reconcile tool availability and equipment limits with the design.
- Maintained the time and cost estimate and the risk register for each program. Prepared contingency sections for losses, stuck pipe and pressure deviations, including the well-control inputs required for technical approval.
- Supported 8 of these wells during execution. Used daily reports and service observations to identify deviations, compare response options and prepare documented changes for approval.
- Completed end-of-well analyses of nonproductive time, tool performance and estimate variance. Introduced a common issue log so unresolved actions remained visible during the next planning cycle.
