# H10

## Professional profile

Well engineer with experience from January 2017 to August 2026 on offshore gas projects. The main responsibility has been connecting well design decisions to rig execution, with particular attention to equipment limits, pressure uncertainty and interfaces between service teams.

## Experience

### Well Planning Engineer — Northbank Offshore Engineering
January 2021–August 2026

- Developed drilling design packages for 8 offshore gas wells, covering the trajectory, casing, cementing, drilling fluid, hydraulics and well-control planning sections.
- Compared alternative casing schemes against the geological pressure scenarios supplied by the subsurface team. Ran casing load calculations and documented the cases requiring further specialist review.
- Worked with the directional team on landing depth and dogleg constraints, reviewed anti-collision results, and reconciled the chosen trajectory with bottom-hole assembly and casing-running limitations.
- Modeled circulating pressure losses and equivalent circulating density. Used the results with the fluids team to review fluid density and rheology options within the design assumptions.
- Coordinated cement volumes, placement sequence and verification tasks with the service engineer. Carried interface actions into the issued drilling program.
- Prepared torque and drag forecasts for the proposed drillstring, compared them with equipment capacities, and recorded the assumptions used for the operating envelope.
- Produced time and cost forecasts and led multidisciplinary reviews of losses, wellbore instability and pressure uncertainty. Documented the well-control planning inputs for approval by the responsible technical authority.
- Supported execution of 6 wells from the operations office, checked actual performance against the design, and prepared change requests when observed conditions differed from the planning basis.
- Investigated nonproductive time and closed out end-of-well reviews with the rig and service teams. Maintained a lessons register used in the next planning cycle.
