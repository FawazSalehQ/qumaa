Drilling Engineer — Onshore Oil & Gas
Location: Saudi Arabia; office-based engineering with scheduled rig-site visits.

We need a Drilling Engineer to plan and support safe, efficient construction of onshore oil and gas wells. The role covers planning, operational support and post-well review, working with subsurface, rig and service-company teams.

Responsibilities
- Prepare integrated drilling programs and review offset-well lessons, trajectories and formation risks.
- Design or technically review casing strings and cementing programs; document assumptions, load cases and contingencies.
- Evaluate drilling fluids, drilling hydraulics, equivalent circulating density, hole cleaning, torque and drag, and bottom-hole assemblies.
- Provide daily engineering support to rig operations; investigate deviations, coordinate technical changes and maintain approved program revisions.
- Contribute to well control planning, barrier reviews, risk assessment and mitigation of lost circulation, stuck pipe and other drilling hazards.
- Prepare authorization for expenditure (AFE) estimates, track time and cost, analyze non-productive time (NPT), and turn end-of-well findings into practical improvements.
- Communicate engineering recommendations and supporting evidence clearly to multidisciplinary teams.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in petroleum, mechanical, drilling or another relevant engineering discipline.
- At least five years of direct drilling engineering experience in oil and gas, including onshore well planning and execution support.
- Demonstrated engineering responsibility across well programs, casing/cementing, hydraulics and torque-and-drag analysis, with documented safety and operational decisions.
- Practical experience with engineering calculations, technical reporting and time/cost analysis. Comparable engineering software experience is acceptable; no particular brand is required.

Current relevant well-control training and experience with directional or horizontal wells are useful supporting evidence, not substitutes for engineering experience. Assess applications by demonstrated work and responsibilities. The language used to write a CV is not a selection criterion.
