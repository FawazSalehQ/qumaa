# C11

## Profile
Well Construction Engineer with nine years of engineering responsibility on land-based oil and gas developments. I manage the link between design assumptions and field execution, including deviated wells with extended lateral sections. My experience covers technical assurance, operating support and cost and performance reviews.

## Work history
### Well Construction Engineer — Sandarc Resources | 2019–2026
- Authored well execution plans for 21 land wells, integrating trajectory, tubular design, cement placement, mud properties, bit selection and BHA recommendations.
- Checked burst, collapse and axial loading for casing and liners, and agreed cement volumes, displacement limits and contingency arrangements with service specialists.
- Modeled circulating pressure losses, ECD, transport of drilled cuttings and string friction. Used hookload trends to calibrate torque/drag assumptions before approving operating changes.
- Provided daily technical support to two rigs, issuing documented program revisions when measured conditions differed from the design basis.
- Facilitated barrier assurance and pre-spud hazard reviews. Prepared response options for circulation losses and stuck pipe with explicit triggers for technical escalation and management review.
- Built authorization-for-expenditure estimates, maintained time-depth and cost forecasts and challenged unexplained differences between daily reports and invoices.
- Led end-of-well performance reviews, traced nonproductive time to equipment and process causes, and transferred corrective actions into subsequent well plans.

### Drilling Engineer — Stonebend Technical Services | 2017–2019
Supported onshore well planning and rig execution. Developed hydraulic calculations, reviewed casing design inputs and prepared well closeout reports under peer review.
