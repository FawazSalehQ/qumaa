# Skill vocabulary notes

## Drilling and well engineering — 2026-09-23

Nine concepts were added to address the previously uncovered drilling domain:
well planning, casing design, well cementing, drilling fluids, drilling
hydraulics, torque and drag, well control, directional drilling and bottomhole
assemblies. These are searchable skill terms, not evidence of seniority,
certification, operational ownership or a recommendation to hire.

Primary references checked for the concepts and terminology:

- [SLB DrillPlan](https://www.slb.com/products-and-services/delivering-digital-at-scale/software/delfi/delfi-solutions/drillplan)
  and its [engineering features](https://www.slb.com/products-and-services/delivering-digital-at-scale/software/delfi/delfi-solutions/drillplan/DrillPlan-Features)
  describe the planning and engineering disciplines.
- SLB's glossary defines [casing](https://glossary.slb.com/terms/c/casing),
  [primary cementing](https://glossary.slb.com/terms/p/primary_cementing),
  [well control](https://glossary.slb.com/terms/w/well_control),
  [directional drilling](https://glossary.slb.com/terms/d/directional_drilling)
  and [bottomhole assemblies](https://glossary.slb.com/terms/b/bottomhole_assembly).
- The [Lebanese Petroleum Administration's Arabic industry description](https://www.lpa.gov.lb/arabic/in-country-value-creation/investment-promotion)
  supports the Arabic fluids terminology and distinguishes drilling, reservoir,
  production and other service activities.
- [3t Drilling Systems' Arabic simulator description](https://www.3tglobal.com/ar/3t-drilling-systems/drillsim20/)
  uses the Arabic well-control terminology in the drilling context.
- [Syrian Private University's petroleum-engineering course description](https://www.spu.edu.sy/petrol/index.php?act=0&cat_id=2346&dir=items&ex=2&lang=1&page=show&ser=62)
  describes casing and cementing in Arabic. The search index excerpt was
  accessible during review; direct page retrieval failed.

Arabic equivalents and orthographic variants also include authored translations;
the sources do not certify every alias. An independent bilingual drilling-domain
reviewer still needs to check regional usage, missed terms and ambiguous aliases.
The list intentionally omits unqualified words such as drilling, cement, fluids,
hydraulics, pressure, risk and emergency, and short abbreviations such as BHA,
AFE, ECD and NPT on their own. Specific skills can still be shared across
professions; term detection cannot establish who performed the work or interpret
negation. Ranking evidence and human review remain necessary.

The unqualified Arabic emergency and sorting aliases were removed from Emergency
Care; explicit nursing, medical triage and emergency-department phrases remain.
Regression tests use independently authored English/Arabic engineering,
specialist, civil, reservoir, mechanical, reporting and clinical snippets. Frozen
pilot candidates, postings and labels were not changed to accommodate this list.
